# Data

## Dataset availability

The dataset used in the Fetal-CARE study is privately acquired from a tertiary scan center and is not redistributed with this repository.

The study consists of:

- **Cohort A:** 500 patient examinations for initial model development
- **Cohort B:** 1,500 patient examinations and 22,987 images for comparative evaluation
- **20 standard fetal ultrasound planes** recommended by ISUOG

Cohort A uses a patient-wise 80:20 training-validation split.

Cohort B is used for comparative evaluation of:

1. Conventional AI-assisted Verification (CAV)
2. Fetal-CARE
